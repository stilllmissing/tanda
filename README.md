# tanda.

TANDA is a concept for a bilingual (English/Spanish), digital-first U.S. consumer banking platform. It would be a nonbank fintech working with an FDIC-insured partner bank. Its brand is rooted in Mexican/Latino culture (mercado, panadería, neighborhood commerce), and the product is built for customers of every background and age.

> **Status:** planning stage. These documents are founder-approved product and marketing plans. They do not show live availability, signed partnerships, regulatory approval, or validated demand. Live-money features need partner-bank and compliance sign-off.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Launch geography | California and Texas, digital-only |
| Platforms | iOS, Android, and full web banking at public launch |
| Banking model | Nonbank fintech + FDIC-insured partner bank |
| Launch policy | Keep every founder-approved launch feature. Extend the timeline instead of deferring features (decision of Oct 16, 2026) |
| Explicit exclusions | Traditional rotating tandas and collaborative pooled savings |

**First-release scope:** checking and savings; direct deposit and external funding; physical and virtual debit cards; savings automation; TANDA-to-TANDA payments; TANDA Assist; TANDA Shield; Trusted Helper; joint accounts and Family tools; TANDA+ membership ($7.99/mo, waived with qualifying direct deposit); TANDA Rewards.

**Three brand pillars:** (1) simple, bilingual, accessible banking; (2) transparent fees and everyday value; (3) cultural recognition, community trust, and belonging.

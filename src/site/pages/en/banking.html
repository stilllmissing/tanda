Checking

See what you can spend, first

  • Direct depositGet your routing and account numbers in the app for payroll and benefits.
  • Clear transaction statusPending, completed or held, with a plain explanation for each.
  • Statements and documentsDownload statements and account documents any time.

Savings

Savings goals with names, not numbers

Open an individual savings account and give each goal a name, like "Quinceañera," "Car repair" or "Rainy day." Then let it grow on its own.

  • Named goalsTrack progress toward each goal separately.
  • Automatic transfersMove a set amount on the schedule you choose.
  • Round-upsRound eligible debit purchases up to the next dollar and save the change.
  • InterestAny interest rate will depend on our partner bank's savings product and will be disclosed before you open an account.10

Debit cards

A virtual card right away, a physical card in the mail

Once you're approved, use your virtual card in the app while your physical card ships. Both are in your control.

  • Lock and unlockFreeze your card in one tap if you misplace it.
  • Purchase alertsA notification for every card transaction.
  • Lost or stolenReport it in the app and request a replacement.
  • Free in-network ATM withdrawalsAt participating ATMs in our network. Out-of-network fees may apply.4

Moving money

Know when your money will arrive

Every transfer shows its type, cost and expected timing before you confirm. An internal TANDA payment, a bank transfer and a card purchase are always labeled differently.

  • Link an outside bank with PlaidConnect and verify your other accounts with your consent, and disconnect them any time.15
  • Free standard ACH transfersMove money to and from your linked accounts at no cost.
  • Optional instant transfersFaster, for a fee shown before you confirm, along with limits and timing.5
  • Send to other TANDA membersConfirm exactly who you're paying before money moves.

If you come up short

Two optional ways to cover a shortfall

You choose which one goes first. We'll recommend an order and explain why, and you can change it in settings.

TANDA Spot

Up to $50

Fee-free overdraft coverage for eligible customers

No overdraft fee and no interest on covered amounts. Your approved limit may be lower than $50, and coverage isn't guaranteed. You'll always see your limit and how repayment works.6

Savings Backup

$0

Use your own savings to cover the gap

If you turn it on, we move available money from your TANDA savings to checking to cover a qualifying shortfall. No transfer fee, and you can turn it off any time.7

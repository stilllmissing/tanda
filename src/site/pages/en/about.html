About TANDA

Modern banking with the warmth of a community institution

We're building a bilingual, digital-first way to bank that's precise about money and warm about people.

Why we exist

Banking apps shouldn't make you feel lost

Too many banking apps bury essential tasks behind technical language and unfamiliar steps. People who prefer Spanish, older customers and anyone new to digital banking often end up asking a relative for help, or giving up.

TANDA starts from a different idea. Every financial action should be explained. Help should be available in your language without embarrassment. And a trustworthy financial product can still feel like home.

Our name

Named for a tradition of trust

In many Latino communities, a tanda is a savings circle built on trust between neighbors, family and friends. Our name honors that spirit of community.

To be clear, TANDA does not offer rotating savings circles or pooled group savings. Your money stays in your own account.

Who it's for

Proudly Latino in spirit. Open to everyone.

Our look and feel come from the mercado, the panadería and the neighborhood store. Our product is for people of every background and every age: students, working families, multigenerational households and grandparents who want to bank on their own terms.

How we work

Our commitments

Clarity

Explain every action

Balances, fees, payment status, risk and ownership are always stated plainly.

Language

Bilingual by design

Essential features and human support work equally in English and Spanish.

Autonomy

Your money, your decisions

Family can guide you, but nobody can move your money or approve activity for you.

Honesty

No overpromising

We say what's planned, what's conditional and what we don't know yet.

Culture

Original art, credited artists

We plan to commission artists and license designs rather than borrow imagery.

Community

Local partners

We plan to work with neighborhood businesses, community organizations and educators in California and Texas.

Help & safety

Help when you want it. Control that stays with you.

Ask a question in your language, get a warning before a scam, and invite family to guide you without ever giving up control of your money.

TANDA Assist

Tap “?” on any major screen

Ask by typing or speaking, in English or Spanish. With your permission, Assist explains what's on your screen and highlights the next step, so you can finish the task yourself. If you'd rather talk to a person, it connects you to a bilingual agent.

What Assist will never do

  • Move your moneyIt can't send payments or start transfers.
  • Approve anything for youEvery financial action needs your own confirmation.
  • Ask for your secretsIt will never ask for your password, PIN or one-time code.

Assist uses AI and tells you so. It gives information, not financial advice.12

TANDA Shield

Warnings that make sense

When a payment or login looks unusual, Shield explains the risk in plain language and suggests how to check it independently. Some activity may need an extra verification step or a short pause. If we restrict something, we'll tell you why and how to get help.

  • Emergency LockLock your card, sign out your devices and report unauthorized activity from one place.
  • Strong sign-inPasskeys, biometrics and extra checks for sensitive changes.
  • Safe recoveryGet back in after a lost phone with identity verification and human help.
  • Honest about limitsNo system can stop every scam. Shield is one layer of protection.13

Trusted Helper

Family can guide you. Only you decide.

Invite a son, granddaughter or friend to help you find your way around the app and recover access if you get locked out. You choose what they can help with and can remove them at any time.

A Trusted Helper cannot

  • See your passwords or codesYour sign-in stays private.
  • Move money or approve activityThey can't send, withdraw or authorize anything.
  • Act as youThey can't reset your credentials or sign in on your behalf.

Need shared access to money? Eligible joint accounts are a separate option where both owners can withdraw.14

TANDA is committed to making our website and future banking services usable by everyone, including people with disabilities and people of every age.

1. Our commitment

Accessibility is part of how we design, not a separate mode. We aim for one clear experience that works with screen readers, keyboards, magnification and voice input, in both English and Spanish.

2. Standard we follow

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

3. What we've done

  • Readable type at comfortable sizes, with text that can be enlarged without loss of content.
  • Color contrast checked against WCAG thresholds in light and dark modes.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Semantic headings, labeled form fields and descriptive error messages.
  • Reduced motion for visitors who request it in their device settings.
  • Equivalent content in English and Spanish, with the page language identified for assistive technology.

4. Known limitations

This website has not yet completed an independent accessibility audit. We plan to complete one, including testing with assistive technology users, before accounts open. Some decorative artwork is hidden from assistive technology on purpose.

5. Report a barrier

If you have trouble using any part of this website, please tell us what page you were on and what happened. We'll work to fix it and to get you the information you need in another format.

Summary. On this website we collect only what we need to run the waitlist and the site: your email address, optional first name, state and language preference, plus basic technical information. We use it to send you updates. We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

This notice covers the TANDA website and waitlist only. When TANDA accounts become available, a separate privacy notice from TANDA and its partner bank will describe how account information is handled under federal financial privacy law.

1. Who we are

This privacy notice explains how TANDA ("TANDA," "we," "us" or "our") handles personal information collected through this website and our waitlist (together, the "Services"). For questions, see Contact us.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Waitlist sign-up: email address, first name (optional), state of residence and preferred language.
  • Communications: information you include if you email us or reply to our messages.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, Social Security numbers, Individual Taxpayer Identification Numbers, bank or card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information through the Services.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and timestamps, recorded in standard server logs by our hosting provider.
  • Local storage: the website may store your language preference in your browser. It stays on your device.

The website does not currently use advertising cookies, third-party analytics or tracking pixels. If that changes, we will update this notice before doing so and provide any required choices.

3. How we use information

  • To add you to the waitlist and send launch updates in your preferred language.
  • To understand interest by state so we can plan our launch.
  • To operate, secure and troubleshoot the website, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only as described here:

  • Service providers who host the website, send email or store data on our behalf, under contracts that limit their use of the information to providing services to us.
  • Legal and safety purposes, when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of TANDA, our users or others.
  • Business transfers, in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this notice.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Data retention

We keep waitlist information until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until it is no longer needed for the waitlist, whichever comes first. Server logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting. We may keep information longer where the law requires it.

6. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe: every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Language: you can change your preferred language by contacting us.
  • Global Privacy Control: we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because we do not sell or share personal information, no further action is needed.

7. California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, gives you the right to:

  • Know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the purposes and the categories of recipients.
  • Delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information through the Services.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

8. Texas privacy rights

If you are a Texas resident, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act gives you the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access, correct, delete and obtain a portable copy of it. You also have the right to opt out of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising and certain profiling. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising or such profiling.

If we decline to act on your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision within a reasonable time. We will respond to your appeal in writing within 60 days. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may contact the Texas Attorney General.

9. Other U.S. state rights

Residents of other states may have similar rights under their state's law. We will honor applicable requests in accordance with that law.

10. How to exercise your rights

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details in Contact us and tell us which right you want to exercise. We will not ask for sensitive information to verify your request.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encrypted connections to this website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children

The Services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or from anyone under 16 for sale or sharing. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Third-party links

The Services may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this notice.

14. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. We will post the updated notice here and change the "Last updated" date. If we make material changes, we will provide additional notice as required by law.

TANDA is a financial technology company, not a bank. TANDA accounts are not yet available. The products, features, fees and benefits described on this website are planned, have not been approved by a bank partner or regulator, and may change or not be offered.

Numbered references on our website (for example, 6) correspond to the numbered sections below.

1. Bank status and partner bank

TANDA is a financial technology company and is not a bank. Banking services, including deposit accounts and debit cards, are expected to be provided by an FDIC-insured partner bank. TANDA will identify its partner bank on this website and in account documents once a partnership agreement has been executed and approved.

TANDA does not hold a bank charter and does not itself accept deposits.

2. Planned products and features

All products and features described on this website, including checking and savings accounts, debit cards, transfers, TANDA Spot, Savings Backup, TANDA+, TANDA Rewards, TANDA Assist, TANDA Shield, Trusted Helper, joint accounts and household budgeting tools, are in development. Their availability depends on partner-bank approval, regulatory review, vendor agreements and testing. Features may launch at different times, differ from their descriptions on this website, or not launch.

Nothing on this website is an offer to open an account or an offer of credit. Accounts will be subject to the terms of an account agreement and related disclosures provided before account opening.

3. Fees and pricing

Pricing on this website is planned and is not final. TANDA's goal is to offer core checking and savings without a monthly maintenance fee. Final fees, limits and conditions will be stated in the account agreement and fee schedule, which will control over any description on this website. Any fee will be disclosed before you complete the transaction to which it applies.

4. ATM withdrawals

Free withdrawals are planned at participating ATMs in TANDA's designated network. The ATM network has not yet been selected. Withdrawals at out-of-network ATMs may be subject to fees charged by TANDA or its partner bank, and ATM operators may charge their own surcharges. Withdrawal limits will apply. Free ATM withdrawals do not mean that cash deposits are available; cash deposit options are being evaluated separately.

5. Transfers and instant transfers

Standard ACH transfers to and from linked external accounts are planned to be free. Standard ACH transfers typically take one to three business days, and timing may vary. Optional instant transfers may carry a fee. The fee, eligibility, limits and estimated delivery time will be shown before you confirm. Transfers may be delayed, held or declined for risk, verification or compliance reasons. TANDA-to-TANDA payments are available only between eligible TANDA customers and are subject to limits.

6. TANDA Spot

TANDA Spot is planned fee-free overdraft coverage for eligible checking customers. Eligibility criteria have not been finalized and not all customers will qualify. Eligible customers will receive an individual coverage limit of no more than $50, which may be lower. No overdraft fee or interest will be charged on covered amounts.

TANDA Spot covers only eligible transaction types. Transactions that exceed your available balance plus your remaining TANDA Spot limit may be declined. Coverage is not guaranteed, and TANDA may change or end your limit as permitted by your account terms. Repayment terms will be disclosed before you enroll. TANDA Spot is not a loan or line of credit.

7. Savings Backup

Savings Backup is an optional planned service that moves available funds from your own eligible TANDA savings account to your TANDA checking account to cover a qualifying shortfall. It requires your affirmative authorization and can be turned off at any time. Transfers are limited to the available balance in your savings account. No transfer fee is planned. You may choose whether Savings Backup or TANDA Spot applies first.

8. TANDA+ membership

TANDA+ is a planned optional membership priced at $7.99 per month. TANDA plans to waive the monthly fee for any measurement period in which your qualifying direct deposits total at least $1,000. The qualifying deposit types, measurement period, grace periods and billing terms have not been finalized and will be disclosed before enrollment.

TANDA+ benefits have not been finalized and depend on partner agreements. Specific cashback rates, interest rates and merchant offers will not be promised until they are contracted. Customer support, security features, accessibility features, fraud protections and core banking do not require TANDA+.

9. TANDA Rewards

TANDA Rewards is a planned program of merchant-funded offers. Basic offers are planned for all eligible customers, and enhanced offers for TANDA+ members. Participating merchants, offer terms, eligibility, reward amounts and redemption rules will be disclosed with each offer. Offers are subject to change and availability.

10. Savings and interest

Whether a TANDA savings account earns interest, and at what rate, depends on the partner bank's savings product. No annual percentage yield is advertised or promised on this website. Any rate will be disclosed before account opening and may change.

11. Eligibility and identity verification

Account opening will be subject to identity verification, sanctions screening and the partner bank's customer identification program. TANDA intends to accept applications using a Social Security number or an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number where the partner bank's policies and applicable law allow. Approval is not guaranteed. Initial availability is planned for residents of California and Texas.

12. TANDA Assist

TANDA Assist is a planned in-app help feature that uses artificial intelligence to explain screens and guide you through tasks. It provides general information, not financial, legal or tax advice. It cannot initiate, approve or change transactions, bypass authentication or access your passwords, PINs or one-time codes. Responses may be incomplete or inaccurate; your account agreement and disclosures control. You can ask for a human agent at any time.

13. TANDA Shield and security

TANDA Shield is a planned set of fraud and scam detection features. No security system can detect or prevent all fraud. Shield may produce warnings for legitimate activity, and it may not identify every fraudulent transaction. Transactions may require additional verification or be paused or declined. Your rights regarding unauthorized transactions will be described in your account agreement and under applicable law.

14. Trusted Helper and joint accounts

Trusted Helper is a planned feature that lets an account owner invite another person to provide navigation guidance and assist with account recovery. A Trusted Helper cannot view credentials, move funds, approve activity, reset credentials or act on the owner's behalf. The owner can revoke access at any time. Trusted Helper does not create joint ownership or legal authority over the account.

Joint accounts, where offered, give each owner ownership of and independent withdrawal authority over the account. Joint accounts and household budgeting tools are subject to partner-bank approval.

15. Linking external accounts

TANDA plans to use Plaid Inc. to connect and verify external bank accounts with your consent. Your use of Plaid will be subject to Plaid's own terms and privacy policy. Not all financial institutions are supported, and alternative verification methods will be offered where available. You can disconnect linked accounts at any time.

16. Customer support availability

TANDA plans to offer live chat in English and Spanish 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and bilingual phone support during published business hours. Support availability may be limited during maintenance, outages or periods of high demand. Hours and contact methods will be published before accounts open.

Planned for launch

Everything you need for everyday money

These features are planned for our first public release on iOS, Android and the web. Each one depends on approval from our partner bank and on regulatory review.

Accounts

Checking and savings

See your available balance first. Set up direct deposit, name your savings goals, schedule automatic transfers and round up eligible card purchases.

Cards

Physical and virtual debit cards

Use a virtual card in the app right after approval while your physical card ships. Lock or unlock it any time and get an alert for every purchase.

Moving money

Transfers with clear timing

Link an outside bank with Plaid. Standard ACH transfers are free. You can choose an instant transfer for a fee, and you'll see the fee and timing before you confirm. Send money to other TANDA members with a clear check of who you're paying.

Help

TANDA Assist

Tap “?” and ask by typing or speaking, in English or Spanish. Assist shows you the next step on screen, or connects you with a bilingual person. It can explain, but it can never move your money or ask for your PIN.

Safety

TANDA Shield

Get scam and fraud warnings in plain language when something looks unusual. If your phone goes missing, Emergency Lock lets you lock your card and sign out your devices in one place.

Family

Trusted Helper and joint accounts

Invite someone you trust to guide you through the app. They can help you find your way but can never see your passwords, move your money or approve anything for you. Eligible joint accounts and household budgeting are planned too.

Questions

Straight answers

Is TANDA a bank?

No. TANDA is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services will be provided by an FDIC-insured partner bank. We'll name our partner bank once the agreement is final.

Will my money be FDIC insured?

Deposits will be held at our partner bank. FDIC insurance details, including coverage limits and conditions, will be shared in the account disclosures before accounts open.

When can I open an account?

We haven't set a launch date. We plan to open in California and Texas first, after our partner bank and regulators sign off. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know.

Who can apply?

TANDA is for people of every background and every age. We're working to accept applications with an SSN or an ITIN where our partner bank's rules allow. Every application goes through identity verification, and approval is not guaranteed.

Does TANDA run a tanda or savings circle?

No. Our name honors a tradition of community trust, but TANDA does not offer rotating savings circles or pooled group savings. Your savings stay in your own account.

Can TANDA Assist move my money?

No. TANDA Assist only explains things and shows you where to tap. It can't send payments, approve anything or skip security checks, and it will never ask for your password, PIN or one-time code.

What can a Trusted Helper do?

A Trusted Helper can guide you through the app and help you recover access. They can't see your passwords, move money, approve activity or act as you. You choose what they can help with, and you can remove them at any time.

How can I reach a person?

We plan to offer live chat in English and Spanish 24 hours a day, plus bilingual phone support during business hours. Urgent fraud reports will be handled any time.

**tanda.**

**MARKETING REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT**

MRD v1.0 \| Founder-aligned planning document \| October 2026

Scope: U.S. consumer fintech launch in California and Texas. Based on the finalized TANDA product requirements and founder-supplied Mexican folk-art visual references. This is a marketing plan, not a claim of live availability, signed partnerships, regulatory approval, or validated demand.

# 1. Executive marketing mandate

Introduce TANDA as a modern, inclusive everyday banking experience with a proudly Mexican/Latino cultural identity. The brand should feel like the warmth and energy of a neighborhood mercado, panadería, and community gathering place while conveying the reliability, clarity, and care expected from financial services.

Three equally important pillars: (1) simple, bilingual and accessible banking; (2) transparent fees and useful everyday value; (3) cultural recognition, community trust, and belonging. The product is open to customers of every background.

# 4. Positioning, message hierarchy and voice

## Core positioning

TANDA makes everyday money management simpler and more welcoming, pairing accessible English/Spanish banking with a bold, community-rooted identity and transparent value.

## Message hierarchy

- Lead with clear product utility and trust; show the cultural personality as a meaningful differentiator, not a substitute for functionality.

- Explain free standard ACH transfers, free in-network ATM withdrawals, savings goals, and debit-card access precisely and with eligibility qualifiers.

- Explain TANDA Assist, TANDA Shield, Trusted Helper, and human support without implying AI can authorize transactions or that fraud can always be prevented.

- Describe TANDA Spot as up to \$50 fee-free overdraft coverage for eligible users; never suggest guaranteed approval or unlimited overdrafts.

- Promote TANDA+ as optional at \$7.99/month, with a proposed waiver for \$1,000 qualifying monthly direct deposits, subject to final qualification and billing terms.

## Brand voice

Warm, bold, optimistic, and culturally fluent in campaigns; precise, plain-language, and reassuring in financial terms, support, security alerts, and disclosures. Spanglish may be used selectively in campaigns, never at the expense of comprehension. Professional human translation and transcreation are required.

# 5. Visual identity and creative requirements

- Lowercase “tanda.” wordmark and a standalone recognizable mark. Develop a reproducible identity for small mobile icons, ATM locator, cards, and signage.

- Creative vocabulary: vivid folk-art florals, colorful tile-inspired geometric patterns, botanical scrollwork, market-inspired color, papel picado rhythm, and commissioned contemporary Mexican illustration.

- Palette direction: saturated reds, pinks, greens, cobalt/turquoise, orange, yellow, and warm cream; use a controlled, accessible interface palette for account screens.

- Cards: an accessible standard design, vibrant patterned collections, and potential premium artist collaborations; designs must accommodate network/issuer marks and legible card details.

- Use calavera imagery sensitively and contextually; avoid reducing Mexican identity to Día de Muertos alone.

- Do not copy the supplied paintings, stock watermarked art, or the “Mexican Express” card reference. Obtain original commissions or licenses, verify artist attribution and cultural appropriateness.

## Reference interpretation

Founder references span expressive painted portraiture and botanical color, hand-painted calaveras, symmetric floral folk art, modular tile graphics, dark-ground ornamental flowers, and an ornate metallic card concept. They are moodboard inspiration, not cleared production assets.

# 7. Campaign architecture and launch phases

## Phase 0 — Research and validation

Conduct concept, comprehension, brand, and usability testing; verify name/trademark and visual asset rights; approve claims matrix and disclosures; secure marketing partner review.

## Phase 1 — Community groundwork

Build relationships with local businesses, community organizations, bilingual educators, and artists in CA/TX. Produce bilingual educational content and waitlist materials without suggesting accounts are available.

## Phase 2 — Invite-only pilot

Recruit a diverse multigenerational cohort in California and Texas. Test onboarding, funding comprehension, ATM network clarity, TANDA Assist, support, family/trusted-helper boundaries, TANDA+ pricing, and Rewards eligibility.

## Phase 3 — Public launch

Release only after bank/program sign-off and readiness of all founder-committed launch modules across iOS, Android, and full web. Coordinate paid, earned, owned, and community activations. Do not announce an unapproved launch date.

## Phase 4 — Optimization

Improve campaigns by verified activation, retention, trust, accessibility outcomes, and unit economics. Expand creative variants without changing approved claims.

# 8. Website, conversion, and lifecycle deliverables

- Bilingual marketing website with transparent pricing, product explanations, accessible FAQ, privacy/consent information, partner-bank disclosure, and support contact paths.

- Prelaunch waitlist and permission-based updates with clear distinction from actual account applications.

- Product screenshots/videos for checking, savings, cards, transfers, TANDA Assist, Shield, TANDA Family, Rewards, and TANDA+; all must depict implemented functionality.

- App store listing packages for iOS and Android; accessibility and language variants; approved screenshots and eligibility notes.

- Onboarding education: external account linking via Plaid, ACH timing, optional instant transfer fees, in-network ATM use, savings goals, and card setup.

- Retention communications: savings progress, security education, useful product tips, support resources, and membership billing reminders.

# 9. Marketing claims and compliance guardrails

| Claim area               | Required guardrail                                                                                                                                                       |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Bank status / FDIC       | Clearly identify TANDA as a fintech, not a bank; name the actual FDIC-insured partner bank only when contracted and approved. No unqualified “FDIC insured TANDA” claim. |
| Fees                     | Disclose conditions for free in-network ATM use, out-of-network fees, and optional instant transfer charges.                                                             |
| TANDA Spot               | “Up to \$50,” eligible users only, subject to terms; no implication of guaranteed overdraft availability.                                                                |
| TANDA+                   | \$7.99/month and \$1,000 qualifying direct-deposit waiver only with final approved measurement rules and benefit inventory.                                              |
| Savings yield / cashback | No rates, percentages, merchant logos, or savings comparisons without substantiation and current contracts.                                                              |
| ITIN / eligibility       | No guarantee of approval or account opening; follow bank-approved identity verification criteria.                                                                        |
| Security / AI            | No absolute fraud-prevention promises; TANDA Assist does not initiate unauthorized money movement.                                                                       |
| Influencers / privacy    | FTC sponsorship disclosure, consent-based tracking, no disclosure of customer financial data.                                                                            |

# 10. Success measurement and experiment design

| Funnel      | KPIs                                                                                                         |
|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Reach       | Qualified reach, aided awareness, brand recall, geographic/language distribution.                            |
| Interest    | Landing-page conversion, education completion, waitlist opt-in, cost per qualified lead.                     |
| Acquisition | Application start/complete, verified approval, funded-account conversion, CAC.                               |
| Activation  | Direct-deposit setup, first funding, card activation, savings goal setup, first eligible transfer.           |
| Experience  | Task completion, bilingual comprehension, support resolution, accessibility testing outcomes.                |

Reporting must segment by region, acquisition channel, language selected, and accessibility-related test cohort where privacy-appropriate. Avoid discriminatory targeting or eligibility proxies. Set target thresholds before paid launch; do not invent benchmark results.

# 12. Acceptance criteria and outstanding decisions

- All launch materials available in professionally reviewed English and Spanish; accessibility review completed.

- Every financial claim maps to a signed-off product fact, partner approval, and versioned legal disclosure.

- Visual assets are original or licensed; trademark and card network rules are cleared.

- All promised first-release features are operational before broad public marketing.

- Partner bank, ATM network, rewards merchants, TANDA+ benefits, and instant-transfer terms are verified before advertising specifics.

- Marketing budget, acquisition economics, launch timeline, numeric targets, and local market mix remain to be validated and signed off.

Explicit exclusions: rotating tandas, pooled savings circles, teen banking, cross-border remittance, lending/credit products beyond approved TANDA Spot, and investing are not first-release marketing promises.

Positioning-led benchmark and verification checklist

Prepared 16 October 2026 \| Concept-stage planning \| Not a bank-partner approval, legal opinion, or investor offer

# Comparison method

- Competitors below are comparison targets, not verified 2026 feature or price claims. Before investor circulation, validate each fee, APY, ATM policy, cash-deposit feature and Spanish-language workflow against a dated primary source.

- Use task-based mystery shopping: open account in Spanish, recover password, dispute a card purchase, find ATM, add cash, configure savings, contact human support, and add a family helper.

# Competitive set

## Chime

Compare fee structure, overdraft, direct deposit, ATM/cash deposit networks, accessibility, support and actual sponsor-bank disclosures.

## SoFi

Compare breadth of checking/savings, premium economics, interest economics, joint banking, support and cross-sell.

## Varo

Compare bank-versus-fintech structure, account opening, fee disclosures, savings incentives and overdraft.

## Current and Cash App

Compare payments-first customer journeys, cash loading, fraud controls, fee visibility and younger-user acquisition.

## Bank of America, Chase and Wells Fargo

Compare branches, Spanish-language service, cash access, trust and digital account servicing.

## Regional/community credit unions and banks in CA/TX

Compare culturally relevant local trust, bilingual branch services, membership/eligibility and community partnerships.

# Strategic differentiation

- TANDA's potential defensible experience: full bilingual journey before signup, persistent voice/text Assist, permission-limited Trusted Helper, transparent scam warnings, culturally expressive but readable design, and multigenerational testing.

- Differentiation is a hypothesis until demonstrated in usability tests. A colorful card alone is not a durable advantage.

# What not to compete on yet

- Do not promise market-leading APY, zero-cost cash deposits everywhere, nationwide branch coverage, best-in-class instant payments or unlimited overdraft until partner economics and network reach support those claims.

# Research scorecard

- For each competitor record: review date; primary URL; checking monthly fee; ATM network/out-of-network fees; retail cash deposit availability/fees; overdraft terms; Spanish signup and servicing; live support; joint accounts; savings automation; FDIC-bank disclosure; card customization; rewards; source screenshot/quote.

# Positioning recommendation

- Lead with 'banking you can understand and trust' plus bilingual accessibility and transparent economics; use bold cultural branding as a memorable expression of the promise, not a substitute for financial reliability.

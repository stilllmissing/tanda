Mexican mercado energy, serious money management

Prepared 16 October 2026 \| Concept-stage planning \| Not a bank-partner approval, legal opinion, or investor offer

# Brand foundation

- Positioning: an accessible, transparent, culturally expressive banking experience for everyone, with a distinctly Mexican/Latino design language.

- Personality: neighborly, direct, confident, warm, clear. Never infantilizing, exoticizing or assuming every Latino customer speaks Spanish.

- Primary mark: lowercase 'tanda.' wordmark; standalone icon concept must be independently designed and trademark-cleared. Treat all current marks as working concepts.

# Working color system

- Verde Mercado \#006F4E — anchor; Rosa Bugambilia \#E52C74 — campaigns; Rojo Chile \#D52B3A — expressive accent; Amarillo Sol \#FFC72C — highlight; Azul Talavera \#1577C8 — supporting accent; Crema \#FFF7E9 — backgrounds; Tinta \#202020 — primary text.

- Use a restrained subset for account balances, forms, disclosures and financial alerts; reserve rich ornamentation for card art, onboarding illustrations and campaigns. Test WCAG 2.2 AA contrast rather than assuming vivid colors are accessible.

# Art direction

- Commission original symmetrical botanical folk-art patterns, mercado-inspired signage, papel-picado rhythm and talavera-like geometry. Avoid copying supplied third-party card images, Frida portraits, calaveras or copyrighted reference compositions.

- Create a standard card, original artist series and premium TANDA+ editions. Secure source files, copyright assignment or licenses, artist attribution and partner/card-network approval.

# Type and UI

- Choose a readable commercial type family with full Spanish diacritics, strong numerals and accessible weight range. Provide scalable typography, descriptive errors, keyboard focus, reduced motion and clear fraud-warning states.

- Use expressive headings and imagery outside transactional core; keep money amounts, confirmations and risk notices visually calm and unambiguous.

# Bilingual writing

- Offer English/Español before registration, retain preference across web/mobile/support, and commission native-speaker review for financial/legal terminology. Never rely exclusively on automatic translation for disclosures.

- Example English: 'Your money, clearly explained.' Example Spanish concept: 'Tu dinero, explicado con claridad.' Final copy requires bilingual cultural and legal review.

# Brand governance

- Maintain source-of-truth asset library, logo clear-space rules, permitted background variants, accessibility QA, translation glossary, legal claim approval, trademark clearance and card-processor signoff. No FDIC or partner logo until legally authorized.
